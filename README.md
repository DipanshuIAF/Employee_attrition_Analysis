# Employee_attrition_Analysis
In this project, I created an Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard in Power BI to analyze employee attrition and identify the reasons behind employee departures from the company. The goal was to transform raw HR data into an interactive dashboard that empowers HR teams to make data-driven decisions.

The workflow included data cleaning & processing in Power BI, followed by importing data into Power BI Desktop. Using Power Query, I transformed and reshaped the dataset for analysis. With DAX formulas, measures, and calculations, I created KPIs like Attrition Rate, Average Age, and Years at Company. I then designed visualizations using charts, filters, and slicers, and combined them into a structured dashboard that highlights both high-level metrics and deeper insights. Finally, the dashboard was exported and shared as a comprehensive HR report.

The dashboard revealed several key findings:
🔹 Attrition Rate: 16.1% (237 employees left out of 1470)
🔹 Age Factor: Highest attrition among employees aged 26–35
🔹 Job Roles: Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, and Research Scientists had the most exits
🔹 Salary: Majority leaving earned up to 5k, showing pay dissatisfaction
🔹 Tenure: Early attrition within 0–2 years was a major concern
🔹 Promotions: Employees with no recent promotions were more likely to leave

This project strengthened my skills in data cleaning, Power Query, DAX, interactive charts, and dashboard design, while also giving me valuable HR domain knowledge. It demonstrates how Power BI can turn raw HR data into actionable insights that help organizations improve retention strategies and build a more engaged workforce.
